Gauntlet privacy policy

Gauntlet is a self-hosted companion to SparkyFitness that runs fitness challenges between friends.

Health data

  • On Android, with your permission, Gauntlet reads steps, distance, active calories, resting heart rate, sleep and workouts from Health Connect.
  • That data is sent to your SparkyFitness server, which stores it. Gauntlet does not keep a copy of raw health records.
  • Gauntlet stores one daily total per metric you compete on, so it can score challenges. These totals can be rebuilt from SparkyFitness at any time.
  • Friends in a challenge see your totals and rank for that challenge. They never see raw records, routes, or heart rate samples.
  • Health data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything other than challenges.

Your account

Gauntlet keeps your name, email, time zone and an encrypted SparkyFitness API key. Deleting your Gauntlet account removes all of this and revokes the key. Your SparkyFitness data is untouched.

Contact

Questions: contact the person who runs this Gauntlet server.
